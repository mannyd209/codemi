# Changelog

## 1.1, released November 3, 2026

- **App Lock:** lock CodeMi with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, asked right away or after 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes away. While it's on, CodeMi also hides its screen in the app switcher. Turn it on in Computers.
- **Fits large text:** the buttons on a computer's card wrap onto two lines at big text sizes instead of squeezing together.
- **New home:** [mannydlabs.com/codemi](https://mannydlabs.com/codemi), linked from About.
- **More from MannyD Labs:** About links to [Remote Keeper for Claude Code](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/remote-keeper-for-claude-code/id6829386828), a free app that turns Remote Control back on for Claude Code sessions.

## 1.0 (iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo), released November 2, 2026

The first release, [now on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/codemi-kimi-code-remote/id6828491801).

- **Remote for Kimi Code:** full-screen, edge-to-edge remote for the Kimi Code agent on your Mac or PC.
- **Connect in seconds:** scan Kimi Code's QR code or paste its Remote Control link.
- **Every computer, every account:** link several computers and Kimi accounts, each with its own saved sign-in. Label accounts Work, Personal or Hobby. Syncs through iCloud.
- **iPad:** Desktop and Two Computers layouts in landscape, hardware keyboard and mouse/trackpad support.
- **iPhone Duo:** mini laptop mode with a built-in keyboard, unfolded Chat/Desktop/Two layouts, and a cover-screen layout.
- **CodeMi Helper (optional):** turn Remote Control back on with one tap, on Mac and Windows.

## CodeMi Helper

- **0.3.2:** stays connected: notices a dropped connection (after sleep or a network change) and reconnects by itself. On Windows it keeps running when Kimi Code quits or updates. Can be unlinked from one device or uninstalled from the computer for all devices. The installer only links your device when the helper is already up to date.
- **0.3.1:** removing the helper from one device only unpairs that device; the helper keeps working for your others.
- **0.3.0:** works with several iPhones and iPads at once (up to 8).
- **0.2.1:** first public helper for macOS and Windows.
